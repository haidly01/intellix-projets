Riad or villa — how we help you choose

Medina riads bring intimacy and character; peripheral villas bring pools, parking and space for larger groups. We publish indicative ranges on request and never push a property that fails our selection criteria.

What “buyout” means

  • The whole property for your dates — no shared courtyards with strangers
  • Clear sleeping capacity (not inflated listing numbers)
  • Staff briefed for events, not only tourist check-ins

What privatised means here

You take the whole place for your group — not a wing of a hotel. That changes noise, breakfast, and how guests move between rooms and outdoor spaces. Videos on the map help you shortlist; we then check real capacity and calendar.

Many stays pair with a half-day experience (Agafay, Ourika, medina at night) or a wellness slot. Start on the stay map or write via contact.
