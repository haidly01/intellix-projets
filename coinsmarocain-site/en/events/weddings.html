How much does a wedding in Marrakech cost?

Searches such as “Marrakech wedding all inclusive” and “villa wedding Marrakech price” want a range before the first message. All-inclusive here means the same stack as our proposal funnel: venue + menu + entertainment + décor + stays — not just the room.

We place projects in the four venue bands already used in the catalogue — very economical, economical, mid, high. Day-of totals are quoted (dates, headcount, privacy). What is already public is the menu slice: mix formulas below, from €65 to €85 per person.

Very economical

Intimate format, riad or single table, essential menu, quiet décor. Often 20–40 guests, one house rather than a domain.

Economical

Full buyout, Essential or traditional menu, close family staying on site or in satellite villas.

Mid

Domain or villa, Signature menu, entertainment and décor aligned with the house, next-day brunch on the same thread.

High

Signature reception: staging, Prestige menu, day-of staff and timing run end to end.

Venues are not yet classified by band in production: the quote sets the tier with you, rather than a frozen grid. See the menus.
