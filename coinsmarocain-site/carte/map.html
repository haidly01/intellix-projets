More about this map

How to use this map

Filter by theme or zone, open a pin, watch the video. If the place fits, ask for dates and a privatisation quote — without an anonymous marketplace. Exact addresses are shared only once a project is serious.

Coverage is roughly a thirty-minute radius around Marrakech. For longer day trips, see the day-trip guide. For celebrations, open events; for stays, villas & riads.

Why video first

Catalogue photos rarely show Moroccan light, patio scale or neighbour noise. A short on-site film reduces surprises. Only then do we talk budget, capacity and calendar — with a human, not a 40-field form.

Continue with Coins Marocain

Coins Marocain brings together privatised places, private experiences and human concierge service in Marrakech. The video map shows partner addresses; the day-trip guide links medina, desert, Atlas and ocean; events covers weddings, hen & stag and retreats.

For a dated project, send clear dates, headcount and intent. Yasmine replies with a short proposal — not a catalogue. Contact · Villas & riads.

A curated Marrakech, not a marketplace

We work with a small set of partner places and private operators. That is intentional: fewer options, clearer recommendations, and a single WhatsApp thread from the first message to the last transfer. Videos replace glossy brochure language; privatisation replaces shared hotel floors.

Whether you are planning a couple’s stay, a hen weekend, a leadership retreat or a wedding weekend, the path is the same — shortlist on video, confirm capacity and dates, then build experiences around the house (desert evening, Ourika day, medina night, hammam). French deep-dives remain available on the main site for travellers who prefer them.
